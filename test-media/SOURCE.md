# CCTV test video

`cctv-pedestrians.mp4` is a 30-second H.264 MP4 excerpt from OpenCV's
[`samples/data/vtest.avi`](https://github.com/opencv/opencv/blob/4.x/samples/data/vtest.avi).
It shows a fixed camera view of pedestrians outdoors. The source AVI was
transcoded to MP4 for this project's upload form.
